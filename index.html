Famous Gangsters — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Famous Gangsters is developed and published by SouthGate Software. This policy explains the information used to provide the game, player accounts, online progression, multiplayer features, and account-management features.

Information we process

When you create or use an online Famous Gangsters account, the game may process:

  • Email address and Firebase account identifier (UID) for email/password accounts.
  • Google account identity information made available through Google Sign-In when you choose to use Google Sign-In.
  • Your chosen Gangster Name and selected city.
  • Gameplay and progression data such as XP, rank, cash, bank balance, energy, jail state, owned vehicles, equipment, cooldowns, activity history, and other game-state information required to operate the game.
  • Public multiplayer profile information intentionally exposed by the game, such as Gangster Name, XP/rank, city, jail status, and activity information used by the Players, Ranking, and Jail features.
  • Account creation and activity timestamps used to operate and maintain player accounts and public-player features.

The game does not currently use AdMob or another advertising SDK, and it does not currently include third-party advertising or analytics features.

How information is used

Information is used to:

  • Create, authenticate, and maintain your player account.
  • Save and restore your game progress across devices.
  • Provide online player directories, rankings, and jail/bust interactions.
  • Process server-authoritative multiplayer actions such as jail busts.
  • Detect invalid or conflicting account/profile data and protect the integrity of online game records.
  • Provide account deletion and related account-management functionality.
  • Maintain and improve the operation, security, and reliability of the game.

Service providers

Famous Gangsters uses Google Firebase services for account authentication, cloud data storage, and server-side game functions. Google Sign-In is available as an optional account sign-in/linking method. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies as well as the configuration of Famous Gangsters.

The game also uses Google Play services for Android distribution and platform functionality.

Passwords

Famous Gangsters does not receive or store your Firebase account password in the game's own database. Email/password authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication.

Account deletion

You can request deletion from the game's Settings → Delete Account flow.

You can also request deletion of your Famous Gangsters account and associated data by contacting SouthGate Software at southgatesoftware.team@gmail.com. Please include the email address associated with your account and/or your Gangster Name so we can identify the account. Never send your password.

The game is designed to delete the Firebase authentication account and associated player data when the deletion process completes. Some records may be retained temporarily where technically necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or service integrity.

Children's privacy

Famous Gangsters is not directed specifically at children. The game's target-audience and content-rating settings are maintained in Google Play Console. We do not knowingly request personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Data security

We use Firebase Authentication, Firestore security rules, server-side transactions for selected multiplayer actions, and authenticated account ownership checks to protect online game data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

International processing

Because Famous Gangsters uses Google Firebase and Google account services, information may be processed on infrastructure located in countries outside the United Kingdom. The applicable service providers' terms and privacy documentation govern their own processing and international transfers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under applicable data-protection law, including rights to access, correct, or delete personal information and to raise a complaint with the relevant data-protection authority.

For UK users, the relevant regulator is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Contact

For privacy or account-data questions concerning Famous Gangsters, contact SouthGate Software at southgatesoftware.team@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game's data practices change. The latest version will show the date above.
